title = Profession epics
intro.p1 = A profession epic only leads the way when there's something to do. A profession epic adds its trainer, vendor and crafts to the quest epic when they're close to the quest epic's route.
recipe-colours = Recipe colours
recipe-colours.p1 = An orange recipe always gives a skill point. A yellow recipe usually gives one, a green recipe sometimes gives one and a grey recipe never does.
level = Level it now
level.p1 = <b>Level it now</b> on a crafting profession opens a question with the skill to reach and the way to get the materials. <b>Start</b> then starts the profession's epic.
level.p2 = The epic learns the recipes and trains the ranks at a trainer. At each skill it crafts the recipe that gives a point for the least time spent getting its materials.
level.p3 = When the next rank asks for a higher level than the character has, the epic stops at the cap and waits for that level.
level.p4 = <b>Crafts</b> on the epic's panel lists the crafts at each skill. A key on a material's row under <b>Materials</b> changes the way to get that material.
hide = Hide
hide.p1 = <b>Hide</b> puts the profession epic away while its chores keep coming. <b>Unhide</b> in the Player menu brings it back.

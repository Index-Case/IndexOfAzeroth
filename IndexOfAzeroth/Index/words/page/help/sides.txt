title = Profession epics side by side
intro.p1 = A profession epic started while another epic runs asks whether to run side by side or to replace the running epic. <b>Side by side</b> keeps the running epic as it is and gives the profession epic a panel of its own here and a window of its own in game. Up to two profession epics run side by side at once, and the quest epic then carries none of their chores.
replace = Replace
replace.p1 = <b>Replace</b> sets the running epic aside, and its <b>Back to</b> key opens it again where it was.
beacon = The beacon
beacon.p1 = The quest epic, the Navigation Guide and a loop keep the beacon in game. A profession epic side by side takes the beacon only while none of them runs, and then the closest profession epic has it.
panel = The panel
panel.p1 = The fold key shrinks the panel to its heading and opens it again. <b>Re-plan</b> plans the profession epic afresh from where the character stands. <b>Close</b> closes the profession epic here and in game, and its panel and its window go. Its plan and progress are kept out of sight, and starting the same profession epic again opens it where it was. A closed one opens again only while fewer than two run.

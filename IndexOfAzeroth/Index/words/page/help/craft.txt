title = Crafting epic
intro.p1 = A crafting epic gets every material an item takes and ends with crafting the item. <b>Craft it</b> in Items and gear starts one. The key shows on an item that a profession of the character makes, or one the character has room to learn, and then the epic starts with learning that profession.
materials = The materials
materials.p1 = For each material missing from the bags, the plan buys it from a vendor when one sells it, and buys it at the auction house when that costs less than farming it. Otherwise the plan grinds, gathers, quests or crafts the material, whichever is quickest.
materials.p2 = Each material's row under <b>Materials</b> has a key for each way to get it, and the plan's way is lit. Pressing another key makes the plan get that material that way.
skill = The skill
skill.p1 = When the recipe asks for more skill than the character has, the epic levels the profession to that skill first and then crafts the item. <b>Crafts</b> on the panel lists the crafts on the way.
back = Back to
back.p1 = A crafting epic started while a quest epic runs asks <b>Side by side</b> or <b>Replace</b>. <b>Replace</b> sets the quest epic aside, and <b>Back to</b> opens the quest epic again where it was.

Index of Azeroth sounds

Your own sounds for the page, as .ogg, .mp3 or .wav. Each one saved here is copied as it is into web/audio, where the
page plays it (work/tools/art_watch.py while it runs, or python tools/install_art.py).

The names the page plays:

idle_burst.ogg, idle_burst.mp3 or idle_burst.wav
  Plays once when IN COMBAT bursts: standing idle in a fight at half your health or less. With more than one, the
  .ogg goes first, then the .mp3. None here: no sound.
